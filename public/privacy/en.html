Privacy

Effective 25 October 2026

In short: your keys and seed phrase never leave your device. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking cookies. Our server only gets what a feature you switch on needs.

Who processes the data

Spendrr (spendrr.app). Contact for anything privacy-related: help@spendrr.app.

What stays with you

Your seed phrase, keys, password, balance, payment history and settings are stored only in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB). The seed is encrypted with your password. We have no access to any of it and can't recover it for you.

What our server processes

The server runs on Cloudflare. For each feature, here is what it stores and for how long.

Every request

We use your IP address to limit abuse (rate limits). Daily counters keyed by the network you come from are deleted within 2 days. Cloudflare also keeps technical request logs for a few days.

Payment notifications (only if you turn them on)

The delivery address your browser or phone gives us (push endpoint and its keys), your Ark mailbox identifier, and the block height at which your next coin expires. Deleted when you turn notifications off, when the endpoint stops working, or after 40 days without opening the app. Delivery itself goes through your browser's push service (Apple, Google, Mozilla), or Google Firebase in the Android app. So that a notification arrives right away, a helper server of ours (the relay) watches your mailbox: it knows the mailbox identifier and the permission to read it, nothing else.

@name (only if you pick one)

The name, your Ark mailbox identifier, your Ark address, and when it was created, changed and last active. An @name is public: anyone can look up your Ark address by it and open your profile at spendrr.app/@name. After you release a name we hold it for 30 more days so nobody grabs it right away. A name with no activity for a year can be taken by someone else.

Lightning address (only if you turn it on, beta)

For each incoming payment: the name, your Ark address, the amount, the payer's comment, the invoice and the payment state. We keep these records so you can see what you received. Invoices are issued by our Lightning node on its own server. The payer's network is stored only as a keyed hash for rate limits, and deleted once the invoice is done, usually within 10 minutes.

Cloud backup

Your device encrypts the wallet backup with a key derived from your seed phrase. We only see ciphertext, a version number and a hash of the write token. We can't read the contents. Backups are kept with no time limit. Deleting one from inside the app isn't available yet. It's on the way.

Messages (only if you turn them on, beta)

Messages don't go through our server but through public Nostr relays (see below). With your @name we store and publish your chat key so others can write to you: anyone who knows your @name can find it. Notifications for new messages store nothing: your contacts get the delivery address encrypted with our key, valid for at most 60 days.

Tap to pay

While two phones pair: your Ark address and the timing of the tap. Deleted within 15 minutes. Long term we keep only aggregate statistics with no addresses.

Price, fees, map

Our server fetches these for you from CoinGecko, mempool, BTC Map and MapTiler (map tiles). Those services never see your IP address.

Email

If you write to help@spendrr.app, we keep the conversation for as long as needed. Our email is handled by Google and Resend.

Where your device connects directly

The wallet talks directly to the Ark server ark.second.tech and the blockchain API mempool.second.tech, both run by Second. Second sees your IP address and the data needed to make payments, and follows its own policies. As a fallback the wallet may connect to blockstream.info and mempool.space.

  • Paying a Lightning address or LNURL. The wallet contacts the recipient's service directly (walletofsatoshi.com, for example) to get an invoice. That service sees your IP address.
  • Messages (beta). The wallet connects to public Nostr relays (relay.damus.io and nos.lol, for example). Message contents and their backup are encrypted, but the relays see your IP address and when you send. They follow their own policies, and we can't delete messages from them.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • Features you switch on (notifications, @name, Lightning address, backup, messages, tap to pay): providing the service you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Rate limits and abuse protection: legitimate interest in running the service safely (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

We don't sell data, share it for advertising or use it for profiling.

Who helps us

  • Cloudflare: server hosting and storage
  • Google: email, push in the Android app
  • Resend: sending email
  • A virtual server provider: servers for payment notifications (the relay) and the Lightning address

Some of them process data outside the EU (USA) under standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability and to object. Most features you can switch off yourself in the app, which deletes the related data. Otherwise write to help@spendrr.app. Since there are no accounts, we will need something to find your data by (your @name, for example). You can complain to your data protection authority. In the Czech Republic that is the ÚOOÚ.

Changes

If anything important here changes, we'll say so in the app's "what's new".
