Worth knowing

  • Write your 12 words on paper

    They're the only backup of your keys. Anyone who knows them can spend your money.

  • Keep smaller amounts in it

    Spendrr is a new project and hasn't been through regular audits. For savings, use a hardware wallet.

  • Open it now and then

    While it's open, Spendrr renews your VTXOs on its own. When that's needed, you get a notification.

  • Use only spendrr.app

    Nobody from us will ever ask you for your 12 words or your password.

Questions

Anything to install?

No. On a phone, you add the app to your home screen.

iPhone?

Yes! Add it to your home screen (Share, Add to Home Screen), or Safari may delete its data after a while.

Lost your phone?

Open spendrr.app somewhere else, tap “I have a seed phrase” and enter your 12 words.

Forgot the password?

The password only protects the wallet on this device. Restore it from your seed and set a new one.

What if the Ark server disappears?

It doesn’t hold your money. An emergency exit in Settings moves it to your own address. Takes hours to days, with the app open.

What does it cost?

Spendrr doesn’t charge anything. You only pay the payment fee, and you see it before you send.

Who sees my payments?

The Ark server sees payments that go through it. Spendrr has no analytics or tracking scripts.

Something broken?

Write to help@spendrr.app. We will never ask for your seed!
